Profile

Brandon David Cole is a brand and content executive with more than twenty years in professional post-production, built across reality television and long-form content. He is the founder of VDO Aigency, an AI-powered content production and brand studio, and serves as Fractional Chief Brand Officer to a private family office and its portfolio companies. He attended film school at New York University's Tisch School of the Arts and business school at UCLA Anderson, and works where editorial craft meets brand strategy — the discipline of the edit applied to how companies are seen.
