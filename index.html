Follow Brando is about rebuilding your life after the old version stops working. Southeast Asia is where I’m doing it.

Start here

I didn’t come here
to reinvent myself.

I’m Brandon David Cole. I started my career directing independent films and producing video content for Dr. Dre.

By the time I came to Southeast Asia, I had spent twenty years in film and video production — with editorial credits across Netflix, Bravo and MTV, and years producing and building media operations for major corporate brands. Today I run The VDO AIGency.

Then, almost as a sideline, I started pointing the camera at my own life.

That became Follow Brando.

Somewhere along the way, Southeast Asia stopped being something I was filming and became my actual life. I built a business here. I became a father. I started a family. And the questions changed.

Less “Where should I live?” More “What kind of life am I actually building?”

Follow Brando is where I work through that — money, family, work, health, aging, freedom, mistakes and what happens when the life you built the first time no longer fits.

This is the field journal of the second half.
